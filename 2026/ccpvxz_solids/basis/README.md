The AO basis sets used in this work are in `basis`, the corresponding auxiliary basis sets are in `auxbasis`.

Nonmenclature:
- **all-e**: cc-pVXZ-s for all-electron potential
- **gth-pbe**: cc-pVXZ-s for GTH-PBE pseudopotential
- **gth-hf-rev**: cc-pVXZ-s for GTH-HF pseudopotential
- **ccecp**: cc-pVXZ-s for ccECP

- **dunning**: standard cc-pVXZ
- **gth-cc-pvxz**: the Ye-Berkelbach GTH-cc-pVXZ basis sets, optimized for GTH-HF pseudopotential
- **gth-pbe-molopt**: the DZVP-MOLOPT-PBE-GTH and TZV2P-MOLOPT-PBE-GTH basis sets downloaded from the [cp2k-basis](https://cp2k-basis.pierrebeaujean.net/) website.
- **pob/pob-rev2**: pob-TZVP and pob-D/TZVP-rev2 taken from the [CRYSTAL Basis Sets Library](https://www.crystal.unito.it/basis_sets.html) website.

All auxiliary basis sets are optimized using the algorithm developed in this work.
